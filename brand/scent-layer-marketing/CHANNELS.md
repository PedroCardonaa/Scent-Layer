# Scent Layer — Channels

Scent Layer's marketing investment lives in two places right now: the website and social media. This file is the canonical reference for how each channel should be used — what kind of content, what metrics matter, what to avoid.

## Website

### Purpose

- Conversion. Customers who land here are deciding whether to buy.
- Education. Buyers who arrive curious should leave more confident.
- Discovery. "Find Your Scent" experiences turn browsers into buyers.

### Content Types

- **Product pages** — Hero image, scent name + house, notes pyramid, sensorial description, "who it's for," size + price, batch info if relevant.
- **Blog / journal** — Long-form educational and editorial content. Two posts per month is a healthy cadence.
- **Collection pages** — Curated edits (e.g., "Best gourmands for fall," "Niche starter kit," "Office-friendly picks").
- **Find Your Scent quiz** — Interactive scent matchmaker tied to email capture.

### Blog Content Pillars

Used by `/seo-audit` and `/draft-content` as default angles:

1. **Beginner education** — "What's the difference between EDP and EDT?" "How to make a fragrance last longer." "Top, heart, and base notes explained."
2. **Buying guides** — "Best niche fragrances under $200." "Five vanilla fragrances that aren't basic." "Designer fragrances worth the hype."
3. **Comparison content** — "If you love X, try Y." "*Baccarat Rouge 540* dupes: which actually deliver?"
4. **Trend / culture** — "Why everyone is wearing Parfums de Marly right now." "FragranceTok favorites, reviewed."
5. **Brand deep dives** — Profiles of houses, perfumers, or signature compositions.

### SEO Targets

Core keyword themes to prioritize:

- "decant" + fragrance name (long-tail, low competition, high intent)
- "[fragrance] review"
- "[fragrance] dupe" / "smells like [fragrance]"
- "best [family] fragrances" (gourmands, ouds, citrus, etc.)
- "how to choose a fragrance"
- "niche fragrance for [occasion / audience]"

### Website Metrics That Matter

- Organic search traffic and keyword ranking
- Product page conversion rate
- "Find Your Scent" quiz completion + email capture rate
- Average order value (decants vs. full bottles)
- Repeat purchase rate

## Social Media

Primary: Instagram + TikTok. Treat them as a single content engine — most assets should be repurposable across both.

### Purpose

- Discovery. New audiences find Scent Layer because a video about a fragrance lands on their FYP.
- Education. Build authority by demystifying fragrance.
- Community. Reply, react, build relationships.

### Content Types (Instagram + TikTok)

- **Fragrance reviews** — Talking-head or text-on-video, 30–60 seconds. Honest, opinionated, voice-on-camera.
- **"What's in my decant box"** — Unboxing-style. Strong for new product launches.
- **Explainer / education** — "What does 'sillage' actually mean?" "EDP vs. EDT in 30 seconds."
- **"If you love X, try Y"** — Comparison reels. High shareability.
- **Trend reactions** — Reviewing viral fragrances honestly. Good for reach.
- **Behind-the-scenes** — Decanting process, packaging, the founder talking shop.
- **Carousel posts (IG)** — Education-heavy posts that work well saved and shared.
- **UGC reposts** — Customer "what I bought from Scent Layer" content with credit and permission.

### Posting Cadence (Starting Point)

- TikTok: 3–5 videos/week. Volume matters more than polish.
- Instagram Reels: 3 per week (overlap with TikTok where appropriate).
- Instagram feed posts / carousels: 2 per week.
- Instagram Stories: daily-ish.

### Hashtag Approach

- Don't dump 30 hashtags. Five to seven targeted ones, e.g., #FragranceTok #PerfumeReview #NicheFragrance + specific house/perfume.
- Always include the fragrance name and house as a tag in the caption when relevant.

### Social Metrics That Matter

- Reach and views (especially saves and shares — these signal value)
- Profile visits → link clicks → site traffic
- Email sign-ups from social-attributed traffic
- Engagement rate on educational content vs. product content
- Hashtag and discovery surface performance

### Social Voice Notes

- First three seconds of every video must hook. Lead with the strongest line, not an intro.
- "Hey guys" is banned as an opener. So is "POV:" unless you're using it ironically.
- Captions should add something — context, the price, a witty line — not just restate the video.
- Reply to comments in the same warm-confident voice. Strong opinions are encouraged when asked.

## Channels NOT Currently Active (But on the Roadmap)

- **Email** — Build the list via the Find Your Scent quiz. Welcome series + monthly editorial when ready.
- **YouTube** — Long-form fragrance reviews could complement TikTok shorts.
- **Influencer / affiliate** — Earned-media seeding with mid-tier FragranceTok creators.

When `/campaign-plan` runs and the user doesn't specify channels, default to website + social. Mention email as an option if the campaign would benefit, but don't assume it.

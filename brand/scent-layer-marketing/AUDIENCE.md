# Scent Layer — Audience

Scent Layer is intentionally welcoming. Anyone curious about fragrance is the right customer. That said, three personas guide how we draft.

## Persona 1 — The Curious Beginner

- **Who:** First serious fragrance purchase, or stuck buying the same designer pick from Sephora for years. Often gifted fragrance and wants something more "them."
- **Age / context:** Wide range. Late teens through 50s. Increasingly common among men who've gotten interested via TikTok.
- **Knows:** A handful of mainstream brand names (Dior, YSL, Tom Ford). Doesn't know what "EDP" means.
- **Wants:** Something that smells good, feels grown-up, and gets compliments. A starting point.
- **Fears:** Spending $200 on a bottle they end up hating. Smelling "wrong" for their age or context. Sounding dumb at the perfume counter.
- **How to talk to them:** Explain in passing without lecturing. Recommend a starter decant kit. Lead with vibe and occasion, not technical notes.
- **Example hook:** "If you've only ever owned one bottle, this is the trio that'll show you what else is out there."

## Persona 2 — The Fragrance Explorer

- **Who:** Owns 3–10 bottles. Active in the fragrance internet — TikTok, Reddit, YouTube. Has a wishlist of niche houses they've never tried.
- **Knows:** Top/heart/base notes. Knows their preferences (loves gourmands, hates aquatics, etc.). Recognizes a dozen niche houses.
- **Wants:** Honest reviews of buzzy fragrances before committing to a full bottle. Recommendations slightly outside their usual lane.
- **Fears:** Blind-buying a hyped fragrance that doesn't work on their skin. Smelling like everyone else.
- **How to talk to them:** Trust them with detail. Compare against well-known references. Be honest about polarizing scents.
- **Example hook:** "If you love *Baccarat Rouge 540* but want something less photographed, try this."

## Persona 3 — The Collector

- **Who:** Owns 20+ bottles. Follows specific perfumers (Francis Kurkdjian, Dominique Ropion, Mathilde Laurent). Reads Fragrantica reviews. Sometimes splits bottles with friends.
- **Knows:** Pretty much everything. Has strong opinions on reformulations.
- **Wants:** Access to harder-to-find decants. Honest service. Quality decanting (no leaks, accurate fill, proper atomizers).
- **Fears:** Counterfeits. Poor decanting. Pretentious copywriting that signals the seller is amateur.
- **How to talk to them:** Be accurate. Don't oversell. Mention batch / formulation when relevant. Respect their knowledge.
- **Example hook:** "*Frédéric Malle Musc Ravageur* — current batch, 5ml, decanted from a sealed bottle."

## Cross-Persona Principles

- Use one shared voice for all three. Don't write "beginner content" and "collector content" in different tones — write content that respects all three readers.
- Default reading level: smart 16-year-old. Adjust upward only for technical deep-dives.
- Never assume gender. Fragrance has no gender; lots of customers wear "men's" or "women's" scents regardless of how they're marketed.

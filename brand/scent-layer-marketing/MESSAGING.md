# Scent Layer — Messaging Pillars

Each pillar covers a specific job-to-be-done. Use these as the spine of campaigns, the framing of product pages, and the seed of social content.

## Pillar 1 — Try Before You Commit

**Core idea:** Full bottles are an investment. Decants are how you find what's actually worth it.

- **For the Beginner:** "Don't blow $200 on a guess. Try three for the price of one."
- **For the Explorer:** "That viral fragrance everyone's talking about? Try it for less than the price of a lunch."
- **For the Collector:** "Test current batches before you commit to a bottle."

**Sample headlines:**
- "The bottle costs $340. The decant costs $14."
- "Sample first. Commit second."
- "Five ml is enough to know."

**Avoid:** Framing decants as "settling for less." A decant is the smart move, not a downgrade.

## Pillar 2 — Niche Shouldn't Mean Inaccessible

**Core idea:** Niche perfumery has a gatekeeping problem. Scent Layer carries the houses fragrance lovers are talking about, in sizes anyone can try.

- **For the Beginner:** "Niche just means smaller houses, more interesting scents. Here's where to start."
- **For the Explorer:** "Try Parfums de Marly, Xerjoff, Initio, and BDK without the boutique markup."
- **For the Collector:** "Hard-to-find decants without the hunt."

**Sample headlines:**
- "Niche perfumery, minus the gatekeeping."
- "If the boutique feels intimidating, this is for you."
- "Niche, decoded."

**Avoid:** Talking down to mainstream-fragrance customers. They're not the enemy — they're future explorers.

## Pillar 3 — Build Your Scent Wardrobe

**Core idea:** One signature scent isn't the goal anymore. A small wardrobe of fragrances — for context, mood, and season — is the new way to wear scent.

- **For the Beginner:** "A scent for work, a scent for nights out, a scent for nothing in particular."
- **For the Explorer:** "Add one new genre to your rotation this season."
- **For the Collector:** "Curate, don't accumulate."

**Sample headlines:**
- "One bottle is rarely enough."
- "Build a scent wardrobe, not a collection."
- "What you wear in July shouldn't be what you wear in December."

**Avoid:** Encouraging overconsumption. The wardrobe idea is about intentionality, not hoarding.

## Pillar 4 — Learn As You Wear

**Core idea:** Fragrance is fun when you understand it. Every order is a chance to learn one new thing — a note, a house, a perfumer, a term.

- **For the Beginner:** "Your first fragrance vocabulary, one decant at a time."
- **For the Explorer:** "Know your noses. Spot your patterns."
- **For the Collector:** Less applicable — pillar 2 and 4 resonate most here.

**Sample headlines:**
- "Smell smarter."
- "Every decant comes with a note card. Yes, with actual notes."
- "Wear it. Learn it. Decide."

**Avoid:** Sounding like a textbook. Education is conversational, not academic.

## Messaging Hierarchy by Context

- **Homepage / new visitor:** Lead with Pillar 1 (try before commit).
- **Product pages:** Lead with the scent itself; use Pillars 2 and 4 as supporting frames.
- **Social discovery (TikTok / Reels):** Pillars 2 and 4 perform best — niche education and "did you know" content.
- **Email / lifecycle:** Pillar 3 drives repeat purchase (build your wardrobe).
- **Returning customer / loyalty:** Pillar 3 + 4 (curate, learn deeper).

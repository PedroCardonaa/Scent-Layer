# Scent Layer — Competitive Landscape

Starting reference for `/competitive-brief`. Update this file as the landscape shifts.

## Direct Competitors (Decant / Sample Sellers)

| Company | Positioning | Strengths | Weaknesses / Where Scent Layer wins |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Perfumed Court** | Long-running, encyclopedic catalog | Massive selection including discontinued / vintage | Outdated UX, intimidating to beginners, weak content marketing |
| **Surrender to Chance** | Similarly broad decant retailer | Selection depth, especially vintage | Dated brand, no education layer, low social presence |
| **MicroPerfumes** | Authorized decants from designer brands | Brand partnerships, mainstream selection | Designer-heavy, thin on niche, no editorial voice |
| **Scentbird** (subscription model) | $17/month for a designer decant | Strong brand recognition, easy gateway | Subscription lock-in, weak on niche, formulaic UX |
| **Maxaroma / FragranceX** (full bottles + samples) | Discount full-bottle retailer with samples | Cheap full bottles | Trust issues among collectors; not decant-first |

## Adjacent Competitors (Discovery / Education)

| Company | What they do | Implication for Scent Layer |
|---|---|---|
| **Fragrantica** | Largest fragrance review and community database | The reference everyone uses; Scent Layer should *cite* it, not compete with it. |
| **Basenotes** | Older fragrance forum | Audience overlaps with our Collector persona. |
| **r/fragrance** (Reddit) | Largest fragrance community | Treat as influence source, not a marketing channel. |
| **FragranceTok creators** (Jeremy Fragrance, Curly Fragrance, etc.) | Influencer reviewers | Potential partners, not direct competitors. |

## Where Scent Layer Is Different

- **Voice and education layer.** The Perfumed Court and Surrender to Chance have selection but no editorial. Scentbird has marketing but is designer-heavy and subscription-locked. Scent Layer is the only one combining curated niche + designer decants with a real editorial voice and serious customer education.
- **Modern brand and UX.** Most decanters look like 2008 forums. Scent Layer is built for the FragranceTok generation.
- **Honest, opinionated reviews.** Most retailers describe every fragrance as wonderful. Scent Layer is willing to say "this one is polarizing."
- **Audience-agnostic.** Beginners and collectors shop the same store, served by the same voice.

## Positioning Gaps to Own

1. **The "explainer" position.** No major decant retailer has a strong educational content engine. SEO + social is wide open.
2. **The "honest review" position.** Most retailers are unwilling to call out a polarizing scent. Scent Layer's voice can.
3. **The "modern brand" position.** Better design, better photography, better social presence than incumbents.
4. **The "build a wardrobe" framing.** Most competitors sell sampling as a stepping stone to a full bottle. Scent Layer can sell the wardrobe itself as the goal.

## Things to Watch

- Scentbird-style subscription bundles for niche fragrance.
- Sephora and Ulta expanding niche house distribution (could compress the niche-access advantage).
- Niche houses launching their own discovery sets (Maison Francis Kurkdjian, Parfums de Marly already do this).
- Counterfeit decant operations on TikTok Shop — competitive but also an authenticity-trust opportunity for Scent Layer.

## How `/competitive-brief` Should Use This File

- Treat this as the seed list, not the final list. Web-search before each brief to catch new entrants.
- When generating battlecards, lean into the four positioning gaps above as Scent Layer's wedges.
- Always check whether a competitor is making a move (new house added, subscription launch, social campaign) and update this file when something material happens.
